About

Atlas//Anon is a UK-based producer navigating the shadowed frequencies between wave, witch house, future garage, and post-club. An anonymous atlas — a guide to places that exist only in the space between isolation and release.

Built from dystopian atmospheres, deep bass architecture, and ethereal vocals that dissolve at the edges, the project has carved a distinctive sonic world since 2018. Dark. Cinematic. Intimate.

Music for late-night cities. For the solitary and the searching.
